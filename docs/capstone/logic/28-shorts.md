# 28 Shorts

An optional step inside the Video stage: once the narration's words are timed, the project's text model picks the best self-contained moments; each becomes a vertical 1080×1920 clip (`packages/app/src/slices/shorts/render.ts:26`) with new 9:16 images and big word-by-word captions, and each gets a title, a one-line description and hashtags. It runs beside the render and the YouTube description, fully automatically, with no review checkpoint and no stage of its own. Afterwards one short can be made again, the moments picked again (keeping every clip that comes back on the same sentences), or a clip moved by hand. Paths below are relative to `packages/app/src/` unless they start with `packages/`.

## Trigger & preconditions

- A per-project `shorts` setting, `ShortsSettings` (`slices/shorts/model.ts:6-28`): `enabled` (absent or false is Off), `count` 1–10 (default 3), `minSeconds` and `maxSeconds` 15–180 (defaults 60 and 120), an optional Shorts prompt name `prompt` and Image prompt name `imagePrompt` (absent or blank is the built-in wording, `defaultShortsPrompt` `slices/shorts/model.ts:114-124`, `defaultShortsImagePrompt` `:128-133`). Off keeps the numbers and prompts (`:7`). The seconds each image is held reuse the project's `imageSeconds`.
- Four later optional settings, absent on projects, drafts and templates saved before them and read then as the behaviour before they existed: `titleOnScreen` (absent is off; `defaultShorts` starts it on, `slices/shorts/model.ts:37-43`), `speed` 1.00–1.25 in steps of 0.05 (absent is 1, `shortsSpeedOf` `:85-93`), `musicVolume` 0–100 % (absent is 15, `musicVolumeOf` `:95-100`) and `fullVideoLink` (http or https, at most 2000 characters, `webLink` `:260-268`). Play and Edit project hold the numbers and the link as typed text (`shortsExtrasOf` `:164-178`, `shortsExtrasForm` `:181-188`).
- The background music is a project file, not a setting: the revision content's `shortsMusic`, a project asset id (`slices/revisions/mutation-assets.ts:38`). It arrives two ways:
  - Play: a draft attachment under Outputs → More shorts options → Background music (`packages/web/src/play/stage-rails.tsx:358-380`). Draft conversion keeps it only while Shorts is on (`slices/play-drafts/convert.ts:316-320`); admission checks it is staged and done copying (`checkShortsMusic`, `slices/admission/rules.ts:324-343`); the run start copies it into the project as an asset and names it on the first revision (`attachShortsMusic`, `slices/admission/start.ts:195-208`, `:125-133`).
  - Edit project → Shorts: an upload with `RevisionUpload` destination `{ kind: "shortsMusic" }` (`slices/revisions/mutation-prepare.ts:55-63`, `packages/web/src/project/revision-shorts.tsx:201-230`). The edit refuses an upload while Shorts is off (`slices/revisions/mutation-assets.ts:66-71`) and an id that was never this project's music (`:184-199`).
  - A template made from a project names it "Music from project", to be attached again on Play (`slices/project-templates/from-project.ts:178-180`).
- Hand-set clip ranges live on the revision content: `shortsRanges`, by short number, each `ShortRange { first, last, pick }` where `pick` is the fingerprint of the pick it was set on (`slices/shorts/clips.ts:46-53`). A range set on another pick is ignored (`effectiveClips` `:113`).
- Set on Play's Outputs rail, below the YouTube description, only while the article is not Off (`packages/web/src/play/stage-rails.tsx:353-357`), and in Edit project → Shorts, a section shown while narration is on or Shorts is on (`packages/web/src/project/revision-form.tsx:176-184`). The title, speed, music volume, link and music file sit in a "More shorts options" disclosure whose summary names what differs from the defaults (`packages/web/src/play/shorts.tsx:58-70`, `:121-132`, `:196-205`). Both places show a Shorts style preview (`shortsPreviewOf` `packages/web/src/play/shorts.tsx:38-50`, `packages/web/src/project/revision-shorts.tsx:119`), rendered through `renderShort` by `slices/style-preview/render.ts`.
- It needs narration: `usesShorts` is `enabled && sources.audio !== "off"` (`slices/admission/rules.ts:527-529`); `shortsFields` refuses the switch with narration Off ("Shorts are cut from the narration. Turn narration on, or turn Shorts off.", `:531-551`). With the video not generated, `imageSeconds` is checked for the shorts (`:545-549`).
- It needs the project's text model and image model; the image model must list the 9:16 aspect whatever the video's shape ("This image model cannot make the vertical (9:16) images Shorts need. Choose another image model, or turn Shorts off.", `catalog/validate.ts:77-83`).
- The settings are refused in one set of sentences shared by admission, Play's draft conversion and Edit project (`shortsSettingsProblems`, `slices/shorts/model.ts:200-258`): "Enter a whole number of shorts between 1 and 10.", "Enter a whole number of seconds between 15 and 180.", against both lengths "The longest a short may be must be at least the shortest. Raise the maximum or lower the minimum.", "Choose a speed between 1.00× and 1.25×, in steps of 0.05.", "Enter the music volume as a whole number of percent between 0 and 100." and "The full video link must be a whole web address starting with https:// or http://, like https://youtu.be/abc123. Paste it again, or leave the box empty."
- Shorts prompts are a Library prompt kind, `shorts` (`kernel/db/migrations/0019-shorts-prompts.sql` widens the prompts table's kind check). A picked Shorts prompt is frozen under key `shorts`, a picked Image prompt for the style under `shortsImage` (`slices/project-templates/from-project.ts:132-138`).
- A short-mode project (`mode: "short"`) can't also have Shorts: "A short isn't cut into more shorts. Turn Shorts off." (`slices/admission/short-mode.ts:48-52`).

## Steps

1. Planning adds `subtitles:timing` when captions, the YouTube description, Shorts, short mode, narration-following edits, narration reviews or per-speaker levelling need it (`slices/rebuild/recipe-exports.ts:31-45`); with captions Off it makes no caption files.
2. `shorts:pick` (stage `video`, input kind `local`, operation `shorts-pick-v1`, recipe kind `provider`) depends on `subtitles:timing` only. Its fingerprint values: the timing's identity, the LLM provider, model and thinking mode, the rendered Shorts prompt (blank for built-in), the count, the two lengths, the title, and the project language only when it is not English (`slices/rebuild/recipe-shorts.ts:53-80`). Until its saved answer matches (`savedPicked` `:253-265`), one deferred `shorts:future` (operation `shorts`, token key `shorts:pick`) stands for everything after it; its template holds the pick fingerprint, style, image provider and model, `imageSeconds`, count, `maxSeconds`, the LLM provider and model, and, only when set, the image thinking mode and the establishing image's fingerprint (`:86-116`).
3. When the timing lands the pick runs (`pick`, `slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:82-199`). A missing text model fails first (`:95-99`). The words become numbered sentences (`transcriptSentences`, `slices/youtube/transcript.ts:94-134`): a sentence closes at `.`, `!` or `?`, at a pause of 1.5 s, after 30 s, or where one speaker's turn ends on a multi-voice run. Each line reads `[n] (M:SS-M:SS) text`, with the speaker's name before the words on a multi-voice run (`sentencesText` `:138-148`, `slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:123-130`). The narration's length is the sum of the revision's audio timeline (`:105-106`); a narration shorter than the minimum fails before any call (`:114`).
4. The request asks for strict JSON, best clip first: `[{first, last, title, description, hashtags, why}]` by sentence number (`pickMessages`, `slices/shorts/pick.ts:121-156`), in the project language (`withLanguage`, `slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:136-138`). An answer that is not such a list is a failed attempt the provider wrapper asks again for (`check`, `:132-135`).
5. Each clip is checked in the model's order (`checkPicks`, `slices/shorts/pick.ts:216-315`): sentence numbers in range and in order; start and end from the sentences' own word times (`clipBounds` `:99-119`), opened up to 0.25 s before and closed up to 0.4 s after, never past halfway to the neighbouring sentence; length within [min − 0.01, max + 0.01] s; no sentence shared with a clip already kept; a title of 1–60 characters; a non-empty one-line description; hashtags trimmed of `#`, kept only when one word of letters, digits or `_`, de-duplicated, at most 5, at least 1 (the 3-hashtag minimum is only asked of the model, `:137`, `:277-288`). Up to `count` clips are kept, then ordered as they play and numbered 1..N.
6. When fewer than `count` are usable and there were problems, the model is asked once more with its answer and one sentence per problem (`pickRetryMessages` `slices/shorts/pick.ts:160-178`); the second answer replaces the first only when it held at least as many usable clips (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:144-159`). None usable fails the step (`:160`).
7. Numbering (`keepNumbers`, `slices/shorts/pick.ts:64-90`): a clip on exactly the sentences of a clip in the previous selected pick that carries a seed keeps that clip's number, title, description, hashtags and seed, while the number is still within the new count; the others take the free numbers in playing order and carry the pick's current regeneration token as their seed (null when there is none).
8. The pick publishes `shorts.json` (role `shorts`, the clips without their words or seed) and saves in its payload the clips with their words and seeds, `durationSeconds`, and `sentences` (the numbered transcript's times and words, which hand-set ranges are measured against) (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:171-197`, schema `pickedShortsSchema` `slices/shorts/clips.ts:17-29`). The same publication deselects every output slot and piece key of a short numbered past the new count (`retired`, `slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:196`, applied in `commitRevisionOutputs` `slices/revisions/publish.ts:113-117`).
9. Planning unfolds each clip `N`, with any usable hand-set range in its place (`effectiveClips`, `slices/shorts/clips.ts:102-131`: the model's title, description and hashtags stay; sentences, times and text are the range's). First `shorts:N:prompts`, one LLM call writing exactly K = `shortImageCount(clip seconds, imageSeconds)` image prompts, K = max(1, ceil(seconds / imageSeconds − 0.001)) (`slices/shorts/model.ts:137-142`), from the style and what is said in the clip (`imagePromptMessages`, `slices/shorts/prompts.ts:19-50`). It carries `unfoldsImages` for pricing (`slices/rebuild/recipe-shorts.ts:123-143`). The answer must be a JSON array of exactly K non-empty strings (`checkImagePrompts`, `slices/shorts/prompts.ts:60-79`); its publication deselects the clip's image slots past K (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:228-238`).
10. Once a clip's saved prompts match and number K (`savedPrompts`, `slices/rebuild/recipe-shorts.ts:270-298`, either fingerprint with or without the catalogue's thinking config), planning adds `shorts:N:image:M` (input kind `image`, the project's image choice, aspect `9:16`, the establishing image as `reference` when it is on, and the cast for the prompt) and `shorts:N:render` (operation `short-render-v1`) (`:145-205`). The render's fingerprint values: the timing, the clip number, start and end, the images' identities, `imageSeconds`, zoom, motion style, the subtitle font id, then, only when in use: the `renderExtras` group (title when `titleOnScreen`, speed when not 1, music asset id with its volume, `:212-232`), `["figure-cards-v1", …]` when tables and figures are shown on screen, and the loudness master's values (`:186-195`).
11. A clip's prompts, images and render carry the clip's own token (`clipToken`, `slices/rebuild/recipe-shorts.ts:237-245`): its seed (for a pick saved before seeds, the pick's current token) combined, when set, with the token "Make this short again" renews under `shorts:N` (`shortRemakeKey` `:248-250`). The runner's materialization anchors a clip's prompts to `shorts:pick`, its images and render to `shorts:N:prompts`, and `review:shorts:N` to `shorts:N:prompts` (`slices/rebuild/runtime-materialize.ts:194-198`); a save carries unfolded `shorts:` work over only while the new plan wants it under its own key (`slices/rebuild/transition-repo.ts:64-71`).
12. An image step calls the image provider labelled `Short N image M` and publishes role `short_image` with `meta.short`, `meta.sentences` `[first, last]`, `meta.index`, prompt, provider and model (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:242-279`).
13. The render (`render`, `slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:281-397`) collects the clip's selected ready images in order, the clip's words on its own timeline (`clipWords`, `slices/shorts/captions.ts:61-75`: a word is kept when its midpoint falls inside the clip), the figure cards whose spans overlap the clip (`:307-320`), the levelled audio timeline (`:321`), the bold caption font for the project language (`captionBoldFont`, `slices/fonts/coverage.ts:66-75`: English uses `resolveBoldFont`, which for the bundled Barlow is `Barlow-Bold.ttf`, `slices/fonts/catalog.ts:41-50`), the speed and the checked music (`backgroundMusic` `:423-454`).
14. `renderShort` (`slices/shorts/render.ts:241-303`) cuts the clip's sound out of the video's own timeline (edge silence, intro, gaps, body, outro), joining the segments it overlaps and trimming at the sample (`shortAudioArgs` `:49-123`). At a speed above 1 the narration plays faster with its pitch kept (`atempo`), the word times are divided by the speed (`fasterWords`, `slices/shorts/captions.ts:50-57`) and each image is held `imageSeconds / speed` (`slices/shorts/render.ts:245`, `:275`). With music the file is looped (`-stream_loop -1`), cut to the short, set to its volume, faded in over 1 s and out over the last 2 s, ducked under the narration with `sidechaincompress` keyed by the voice (`threshold=0.02:ratio=8:attack=20:release=400:makeup=1`) and mixed with `amix … normalize=0` at full narration level (`:40-42`, `:92-104`).
15. The images play for their share each with the project's motion, the last running to the end (`shortEditList`, `slices/shorts/render.ts:127-200`); with figure cards, the images take turns around the cards, starting over if the cards leave more shots than images (`:144-179`).
16. The captions are ASS (`shortCaptionsAss`, `slices/shorts/captions.ts:170-228`): two to four words on screen; a group closes at a sentence end, after a comma, semicolon or colon once it holds two words, at a 0.6 s pause, or at four (`captionGroups` `:77-104`). A word left alone joins the group before it (or, when it opens a sentence, the one after first) if that keeps the group at most five words and every line within 12 characters or the group's own widest; otherwise the neighbour's nearest word comes over when the neighbour has three or more; words across a pause over 1.5 s stay apart (`keepCompany` `:112-154`, `fits` `:159-164`). A group stays 0.5 s after its last word unless the next starts first (`:17`, `:191-192`). They are centred at 62% of the height, 7.5% of the height tall, white with the word being spoken gold (`&H0000D4FF`) at 108% (`:20-28`, `:217`). In a language timed by sentence (`wordTimingUnavailable`, `kernel/ports/languages.ts:130-134`) each group is drawn whole with no word lit (`wordByWord: false`, `slices/shorts/captions.ts:194-207`, `slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:348`).
17. With `titleOnScreen` the title is a headline on layer 1 above the captions from the first frame to the last, centred 11% from the top, bold, at the largest of 6%, 5.5%, 5%, 4.5%, 4%, 3.5% of the height that fits two lines inside the 80 px side margins, cut at a word with an ellipsis if even the smallest does not fit (`titleLayout`, `slices/shorts/captions.ts:257-288`, `:29-36`).
18. The slideshow renderer burns captions and title in (`renderSlideshow` with `burnSubtitles: true`, `slices/shorts/render.ts:288-299`), mastered to the video's loudness target when Level the volume is on (`masterGoal`, `slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:339`). The result is measured with `probeDurationMs` and published as role `short_video` with `meta.short`, `meta.sentences` and the master report (`:368-389`). The render's progress is reported as `stage.progress` on stage `video` (`:358-365`).

## Branches

- Built-in prompts versus Library prompts: only the instruction or the style differs; the rules and answer shapes are fixed (`slices/shorts/pick.ts:121-156`, `slices/shorts/prompts.ts:19-50`).
- Hand-edited captions normally skip timing; with Shorts on, timing still runs (`slices/rebuild/runtime-store.ts:48-57`).
- Multi-voice narration: sentences break at speaker turns and the transcript names the speaker (`slices/youtube/transcript.ts:88-93`, `slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:125-129`).
- Non-English project: the pick request is asked in the project language, the pick fingerprint carries the language, captions use a font that covers its letters, and a language timed by sentence gets whole-group captions (steps 2, 4, 13, 16).
- Establishing image on: every short image is drawn with it as `reference` and depends on its step (`slices/rebuild/recipe-shorts.ts:158-161`).
- Tables and figures on screen: the render shows the cards whose descriptions fall in the clip (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:307-320`).
- Edit project → Shorts (`packages/web/src/project/revision-shorts.tsx`): Built-in drops the frozen `shorts` or `shortsImage` template; a Library prompt freezes its body. Each picked clip shows its first and last sentence with Earlier start, Later start, Earlier end and Later end (`:443-449`), "Use my own range" (start and end pickers over the numbered transcript, `:457`, `:491`), and "Make this short again" (adds `shorts:N` to the edit's `regenerate` list, `remakeShort` `:39-41`). "Pick different moments" adds `shorts:pick` and drops the hand-set ranges (`pickAgain` `:34-37`); "Keep the current moments" takes it back. While a count, length or prompt change will re-pick, the clips are locked (`repicking`, around `:268-280`).
- The project page's Shorts part (`ShortsBlock`, `packages/web/src/project/body-shorts.tsx:47`) offers "Pick different moments" and, under each card's More menu, "Make short N again", when the project has revisions and Shorts is on. They open Edit project → Shorts with that change applied (`EditRequestContext`, `packages/web/src/project/revision-action-context.ts:15`), to save and then Remake.
- Copy puts the title, the description with a last line "Watch the full video: <link>" (or "[PASTE THE FULL VIDEO LINK HERE]" without one) and the hashtags on the clipboard (`shortUploadText`, `slices/shorts/model.ts:60-82`); the card shows the line with a "Link needed" badge when unset (`packages/web/src/project/body-shorts.tsx:258-275`). The link is read when copying, so changing it redoes nothing.
- Short mode (`slices/admission/short-mode.ts:5-13`) is not this step: the whole narration renders as one 9:16 video through the same `renderShort`, with the project title on top and captions, role `video` (`slices/rebuild/runtime-export-short.ts:18-115`).

## Unhappy paths

Every message says what failed and how to fix it; the stage prefixes it with the step (`Short N image M: …`, `Short N: …`), which the project page reads to mark the failed card (`failedHere`, `packages/web/src/project/body-shorts.tsx:324-330`).

- No text model → "Shorts need an AI text model to pick the clips, and none is chosen. Choose one in Edit project → Providers, then Try again." (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:96-99`).
- Word timing missing → "The narration's word timing, which the shorts are cut and captioned from, is missing. Use More → Render the video again in the Video section, then Try again." (`:469-472`); empty → "The narration's word timing holds no words, …" (`:101-104`).
- The pick's answer not a JSON list → "The AI model's choice of shorts didn't come back in the expected format …" after the wrapper's retries (`slices/shorts/pick.ts:221-226`).
- No usable clip after both asks → "The AI model didn't pick any clip Slopify could use as a short, twice (…). Try again; if it keeps happening, widen the length range in Edit project → Shorts, or choose another model in Edit project → Providers." (`noPicksMessage`, `slices/shorts/pick.ts:318-323`).
- Narration shorter than the minimum → "The narration is N seconds long, shorter than the M-second minimum for a short, so there is nothing to cut. Lower the shortest length in Edit project → Shorts, then Try again." (same function, no model call).
- Image prompts of the wrong count or format, or an empty prompt → "The AI model wrote N image prompts for a short that needs exactly K. Try again, or choose another model in Edit project → Providers." and siblings (`slices/shorts/prompts.ts:60-79`).
- A short's image missing at render → "Short N is missing one of its images. Try again to make it again; if it keeps happening, use Make this short again in Edit project → Shorts." (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:299-302`).
- A step whose clip is no longer picked → "The clip this short was cut from is no longer among the picked shorts. Use Pick different moments in Edit project → Shorts, then Try again." (`:73-76`); a clip outside the narration → "A short's clip falls outside the narration, …" (`slices/shorts/render.ts:81-84`); no images → "A short has no images to show. …" (`:137-140`).
- Hand-set range refused on save under `content.shortsRanges.N` (`shortRangeFields`, `slices/rebuild/recipe-save.ts:41-62`, words from `rangeProblem`, `slices/shorts/clips.ts:62-97`): "Short N would last S seconds, shorter than the M-second minimum. Start it earlier or end it later.", "… longer than the M-second maximum. Start it later or end it earlier.", "Short N would share sentences with short K (sentences a-b). Choose a range that doesn't overlap it.", "Choose a start sentence that comes before the end sentence, between 1 and S.", "There is no short N any more. Reload the page and choose again.", and for picks saved before ranges existed "These shorts were picked before clips could be adjusted by hand. Use Pick different moments, then adjust the new clips."
- Music: on Play, "The shorts' background music file is no longer available, …" or "… is still uploading, so the run can't start yet. …" (`slices/admission/rules.ts:333-342`, `slices/play-drafts/convert.ts:46-51`); in Edit project with Shorts off "Background music is only used by the shorts. Turn Shorts on in Edit project → Shorts, then add the music." and an unknown id "This music file does not belong to this project. Reload the page and choose the music again." (`slices/revisions/mutation-assets.ts:66-71`, `:196-198`); at render "The shorts' background music couldn't be read as audio (…)", "… file is no longer in the project." or "… file holds no sound.", each followed by "Choose another file under Background music in Edit project → Shorts, or remove the music, then Try again." (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:431-452`).
- A caption font without the project language's letters and no fallback → the font-coverage error naming Edit project → Subtitles (`slices/fonts/coverage.ts:60`).
- A wrongly set-up step → "Slopify hit an internal error (a Shorts step was set up wrongly). …" (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:512-516`).
- Cancel mid-render: the working folder and the pending asset are removed whatever the ending (`slices/shorts/render.ts:300-302`, `slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:394-396`). ffmpeg failures read as the video export's (scenario 11).

## State transitions

- The Video stage is pending → running → done or failed across the render, the description and every Shorts step together; each Shorts step is its own piece, so a failed short fails the stage while everything already made stays published, and Try again redoes only what did not finish (`slices/rebuild/runtime-shorts.ts:49-80`).
- Stale when: the narration or its text changes (new timing); the silence gap or edge silence changes; the prompt, count, lengths, model, title or project language change (the pick; clips it chooses again on the same sentences keep their work); `imageSeconds`, the style prompt, the image model or the establishing image change (the prompts, images and renders); a clip's range is set by hand or "Make this short again" (that clip's prompts, images and render); motion, zoom, the subtitle font, the title on screen, the speed, the music and its volume, the figure cards or Level the volume (the renders only). Not stale when the captions' look, the slideshow images, the Document or the full video link change.
- A pick that returns fewer clips retires the higher-numbered shorts' outputs from selection; their files stay in History until orphan cleanup (step 8).

## Invariants

- Clips start and end on sentence edges measured by the word timing, and their sound is cut from exactly the timeline those times are on (`slices/shorts/render.ts:20-24`).
- A project saved before Shorts, or with Shorts off, plans exactly the work and fingerprints it did before; a short saved before the title, speed, music, seeds, figure cards, loudness or language existed keeps its fingerprints (`slices/rebuild/recipe-shorts.test.ts:135`, `:355`).
- The step never delays the render and the render never waits for it: `shorts:pick` depends only on `subtitles:timing` (`slices/rebuild/recipe-shorts.ts:77`).
- The image count per clip is fixed by the clip length and `imageSeconds`, not by speed (`slices/shorts/render.ts:243-245`).
- A short on screen is the current pick's: a video or image is shown when it was made after the pick or cut from the same sentences as its clip (`currentShorts`, `packages/web/src/project/body-shorts.tsx:284-305`).

## Outcomes & side effects

- Success: `shorts.json`, K vertical images per short and one MP4 per short, shown in the Video stage's Shorts part as a grid of vertical players with title, length, description, link line, hashtags, Copy and Download (`packages/web/src/project/body-shorts.tsx:47-160`), and in the stage's Download menu as "Short N: <title> (.mp4)" (`:84-90`).
- Cost: Play's estimate adds Shorts rows (the pick, one prompt call per short, and `count × shortImageCount(maxSeconds, imageSeconds)` images, `shortsImageUpperBound`, `slices/estimate/index.ts:332-355`); the rebuild review prices `shorts:future` the same way until the clips are picked, and once they are, each clip's prompt call together with its K images before the prompts are written (`priceRecipes`, `slices/rebuild/recipe-work.ts:300-372`).

## Dimensions not in play

- Uploading to YouTube Shorts, TikTok or Reels: the user downloads and copies; no upload code exists.
- Reviewing the picks before the render: the step is automatic; clips are adjusted afterwards.
- Money moved, permissions, multi-user concurrency: single local user; no roles or payments in this scenario.
- Placing images on the passage they illustrate beyond the per-clip prompt call: prompts are written per clip in order, one per `imageSeconds`.
