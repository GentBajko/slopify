# Template studio and designs: fixes

- **Samples spend nothing you didn't allow.** Settings → Template studio has **Samples may use my CLI plans** (off by default). Off, a sample, Compare models or Make samples for all with a step on your Claude Code or Codex plan waits and says why, whatever the daily budget; at $0.00 nothing spends money. On, plan steps run and the budget limits dollars only.
- **Level the volume lands on the level.** A voice whose peaks stand far above its loudness now comes out within half a LU of the target; the studio reads the level of the video's master, and says how far the peak limiter held it back when it can't.
- **Paused schedules.** A paused schedule still uses its template: its runs are listed marked paused, and the checks say it costs nothing while paused and what it would cost a month resumed. Prepare is refused for a paused schedule's runs, on the page and for agents.
- **The save bar stays put.** It sits on the window's bottom edge, shows its whole message, and stays beside the Assistant instead of under it.
- **Designs.** A designed piece's preview shows it as downloaded, with its text, in the format you pick; the bar carries only the open tab's action. A design template is marked ready once its version's sample is made, and Save without a sample keeps any change.
- **Starter packs** ask for `{{Topic}}`, which their template's title and every schedule fill per video. Packs installed before keep working.
