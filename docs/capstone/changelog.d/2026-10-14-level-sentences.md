# Level each sentence

- A new **Level each sentence** switch (Play → Video and style, Edit project → Pauses and volume) holds every sentence of a one-voice narration to the sentences in the minute around it. The voice drifts inside each request (its first sentences a little loud, its last a little quiet, now and then one line well off), and the old step that lowered loud phrases only turned things down, by each phrase's loudest moment. Each sentence's K-weighted loudness is measured and moved toward the median of its neighbours, across request joins too: down by up to 6 dB, up by up to 3 dB and never past -1 dBFS, with the volume changing only in pauses of 0.2 s or more (a part shorter than 1.5 s goes with its neighbour). Sentences are found from the pauses in the voice, since the words are only timed after the join. It replaces the phrase step for the body, and runs before Soften breaths and Soften S sounds. Off by default; turning it on joins the narration again, no speech is made again.
- Measured on a 2 h 29 min narration with the app's own code: the spread of sentence loudness against the surrounding minute fell from 1.0 dB to 0.6 dB, jumps over 3 dB at request joins from 15% to none, and jumps over 3 dB between any two sentences from 3.3% to 1.0%. With the Video volume at −2 dB (−16 LUFS) the final limiter never cuts a peak by more than 3.1 dB (it cuts 3–5 dB about 22 times a minute at −14 LUFS).
