# Posting to X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit and Bluesky (server)

- **Posts per site.** A project's settings (`config.social`, set in the template and Edit project) say which sites it posts to and what each gets: text from the summary, the YouTube description without chapters, the title, your own text with keywords, or a Library **Post** prompt; any mix of video, short, thumbnail, pictures and designs; hashtags and the YouTube link. Each site's post is counted the site's way and cut at a whole sentence when too long, with plain warnings that never block (too many pictures, video too long for X Free, video and pictures on a site that takes one, subreddit missing, release outside the scheduler's window).
- **Post prompts** are paid steps through the rebuild engine (`social:post:<site>`), priced as **Posts** in the estimate, the rebuild preview and Template Studio's Save quote; an answer over the site's limit is asked for again. A project without one plans exactly what it did before.
- **Media in the site's shape.** Pictures are cut to Instagram's 4:5 in a child ffmpeg the first time a post asks for them and kept under the data folder; videos are never re-encoded.
- **Routes.** `/api/social/packs/:projectId` reads and changes a project's posts (its own pick, hand edit, Reddit fields, pasted link); **Fill** queues a post for the extension. The paired extension's `/api/social/ext/*` routes take the next post, its files, due fills and find-links, and report results and the sites it may post on, with the same token and CORS as YouTube Studio's.
- **Channels** gain an X subscription (warnings only) and account names (to find a published post's link). **Library** gains the **Post** prompt kind; the digest and fact keywords and the length work in it.
- **Agents** get six tools to read and prepare posts. Fill and every extension route are excluded, and the assistant is never offered them. `needs_attention` lists a filled post waiting for the person's Post as `post-due`.
- **Backups** carry each project's posts; a backup from before posting imports with none and free X accounts.
