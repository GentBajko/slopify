# The images your video and shorts show are kept in Upload

- Every image the video shows now sits in the project's `Upload/Images/` folder, in order, and each short's images sit beside that short in `Upload/Shorts/<n> - <title>/`. They used to be under `Working/`.
- **Keep outputs only**, **Free space** and **Clean up everything** keep these images now. Before, they deleted them as working files, and making them again never gives the same pictures.
- Projects arranged before move their images into Upload once on the first start; nothing is copied.
