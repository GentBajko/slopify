# Videos are made several times faster, and a run no longer stops by itself

- **Narration and picture checks no longer wait behind the pictures.** Slopify sent at most five calls out at once, across every service. Drawing four or five pictures at a time with Codex left one slot, or none, for the narration and the picture checks, so a video's checks ran one by one for hours and its narration could sit at the start for half an hour. Now up to twelve calls run at once, each service up to its own limit, and the slots of a service are shared between projects: a video's research no longer waits behind another video's 240 picture checks.
- **The app keeps answering while a video is made.** After every finished step (each picture, each check, each piece of narration) Slopify worked out the whole video's plan again, reading most of its saved history; with a dozen steps finishing together the page and the agent stopped answering for up to half a minute. The plan is now kept until something it is made from changes, and the history it reads is looked up by index (two new database indexes, added on the first start of this version). Turning on Soften breaths or Soften S sounds no longer plans the whole narration four times over.
- **Joining the narration is about four times faster.** Its pieces are levelled, softened and given their pauses six at a time instead of one after another, and finding the breaths runs beside the app instead of in it. The files made are the same, byte for byte.
- **A picture that fails its check is drawn again by itself, every time.** The new picture used to wait, held, until someone pressed Try again on the Images step whenever other checks finished at the same moment, or two failed together.
- **Continue the run after a restart takes the picture checks up again.** They used to stay held for good.
- **Redrawing a picture no longer checks the article, the opening or the narration again.** Which of the article's files a check looked at changed from one version to the next, so any new version could make the article's check run again, from its first attempt, and a failing article check rewrote the article (and voiced and drew everything after it) up to four more times. A check now looks at the same file each time, finished checks are kept, and an item already flagged after its last attempt is not tried again unless it changed.
- `SLOPIFY_CODEX_IMAGES_AT_ONCE` (1 to 8) draws more or fewer Codex pictures at once than the four it draws by default.
- Technical: `kernel/runner/queue.ts` (12 total, 8 per provider, fewest-running owner first); `executionPlan` keyed on its inputs instead of `total_changes()` (`rebuild/runtime-plan.ts`), migrations 0079 (`revision_pieces`/`revision_outputs` project indexes) and 0080 (`attempts(work_id)`); `transitionRevisionWork` in two statements; `narration/piece-jobs.ts` and `narration/breaths-worker.ts`; `selectedAsset` sorts a key's files by slot and `settledRecipe` keeps a review made under an older order (`rebuild/recipe-model.ts`); `nextAttempt` keeps the count for the same file (`reviews/outcome.ts`); automatic redos run one at a time per project and try each waiting redo once per chain (`rebuild/review-redo.ts`).
