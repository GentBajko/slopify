# The pan and the zoom are steady at any speed

- **A slow move used to advance unevenly.** The move could only land on a whole pixel of the image it was made from, so one slow enough to travel less than a pixel per frame held for a frame or two and then jumped a whole one. It read as a shake rather than a glide, and it showed most on the long shots, whose moves are the slowest.
- **The picture is now sampled between whole pixels.** Measured on the same image and the same 949-frame pan, the step went from 56.5% off even to 0.7%, and the zoom from 24.4% to 1.0% — at the same render time, and with the same sharpness.
- **The image is enlarged two times rather than four,** since the part of it on screen is already wider than the finished frame and comes down to it instead of up.
