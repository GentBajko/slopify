# Captions during the upload

- **The extension uploads a long video's captions in Studio's upload dialog**, on the Video elements step, instead of on the video's Details page after it is scheduled or published. Press Next to Video elements as usual: Add subtitles → Upload file → With timing → the project's captions file → Done happen by themselves, and a toast says "Captions uploaded." The extension still never presses Next, Save or Publish in the upload dialog.
- If Studio's Video elements step or Subtitles editor isn't what the extension expects, a toast says what was missing and the captions are uploaded on the Details page after the upload, as before.
- A video whose captions went in during the upload gets no Details-page visit for captions; one still opens when it needs an end screen or a related video, and skips the captions.
