# Remove breaths

- A new **Remove breaths** switch beside the pauses between sentences (Play → Video and style, Edit project → Pauses and volume) mutes the loud in-breath some voices take after almost every comma and full stop (Inworld's TTS-2 among them). Words, pauses and captions stay exactly where they were. Off by default; turning it on joins the narration again from the pieces it already has, no speech is made again. See `docs/breaths.md`.
