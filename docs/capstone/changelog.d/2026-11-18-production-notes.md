# A note the AI wrote about its own work is no longer narrated

- **An article no longer opens with the AI model talking about its work.** A model that works as an agent, such as the one behind Claude Code, sometimes began its answer with a line like "I've read all sixteen documents in full and am now writing the narration and its end matter." That line went into the article, its narration and the voice, and the automatic article review flagged it; writing the article again only brought a new line like it. Slopify now drops such lines before the first heading when the article is kept, so `article.md`, the narration text and the review all start at the article itself.
- **The intro and outro the AI writes lose such a first line too.** They have no headings, so only a first paragraph that is plainly a note goes.
- **Real narration is never dropped.** A paragraph goes only when it is short and plainly about the work: the model saying what it read or is writing and naming the documents, sources, notes, article or narration, or announcing the text ("Here is the narration:"). A storyteller's "I have read many old chronicles…" or "Here is a tale of…" stays, and a text is never left empty. An article you paste or edit yourself is kept exactly as you wrote it.
