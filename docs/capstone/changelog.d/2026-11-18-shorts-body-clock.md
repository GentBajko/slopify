# A new intro or outro leaves the shorts alone

- **Shorts are now cut on the body's own clock.** The pick reads only the body's words, timed from where the body starts, and its fingerprint holds the body's narration and how it is timed, not the intro, the outro or the silence around them. A new intro or outro (its prompt, text or narration) moves every word of the video's timing, but no longer marks the pick outdated, nor any short's image prompts, images or render. The render finds the body where it starts now, so a short plays the same words after any intro.
- **A short never reaches outside the body.** A clip on the body's first sentence no longer opens into the silence or intro before it, and one on the last sentence closes where the body's audio ends; the body's own file holds the first consonant and the last breath.
- **Levelled shorts follow the body alone.** With Level the volume on, a short's render reads only the levelled body, so a new levelled intro doesn't render the shorts again.
- **Existing projects keep their shorts as they are.** A pick made before this stays current while the timing it read is unchanged. The first change that moves that timing (a new intro, for example) picks again once, on the body's clock: the finished shorts are kept by their sentences, with their titles, prompts and images, and only rendered again. Every intro or outro after that changes nothing.
- **A keep list sent with an edit that picks nothing again is ignored.** An agent that saved a new intro with `shortsKeep: []` dropped every short; a save now keeps the version's keep list whenever the moments aren't picked again. The agent's save_revision description says to leave `shortsKeep` as get_revision sent it.
- Projects already re-picked by an intro change before this keep their new pick; their old shorts come back only by restoring the version before that change.
