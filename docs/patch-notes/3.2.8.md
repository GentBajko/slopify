# Slopify 3.2.8

Released 2 October 2026.

3.2.8 starts your A/B tests once a scheduled video goes public, tests the other titles too, and lets the pinned comment and other titles be written again.

## A/B tests after publishing

- YouTube only A/B tests public videos, and a scheduled video is private until its time. The Slopify Studio extension now keeps each upload's YouTube link and starts its A/B test by itself once the video is public: it opens the video in a background tab, fills A/B Testing and presses **Set test**. Chrome has to be open around that time; otherwise the test starts the next time Chrome opens.
- A/B Testing now tests the **other titles** too: **Title and thumbnail** with two or three thumbnails, **Title only** with one.
- The project's YouTube section has **On YouTube**: each upload's link (paste one for an upload you made by hand), its A/B test's state, and **Start A/B test** to test any video after the fact.

## YouTube section

- **Write again** is on the pinned comment and the other titles too, not only the description. It still writes every part anew in one AI call; a part you edited keeps your text and offers the new one beside it.

## Upgrading from 3.2.7

- **Download the extension again** (Settings → YouTube Studio → Download), unzip it over the old one and press reload in `chrome://extensions`. It is version 0.3.0, and Chrome asks once to allow its new permissions (a 15-minute timer, and YouTube's public video check).
- **Nothing becomes outdated.**
