# Slopify 4.1.0

Released 7 November 2026.

This release is about making Slopify easy to use without reading anything first: every button says what it does, each screen shows more and wastes less space, and the same thing has the same name everywhere.

## Finding your way around

- **A new rail.** The left rail is narrow, with a word under every icon: Create, Home, Projects, Prompts, Templates, Calendar, Channels (once you have two or more) and Settings, then Search, Assistant, Help and Support Slopify at the foot. On a phone the bottom bar has Create in the middle.
- **Library is split in two.** **Prompts** holds your prompts and intros and outros, with every kind listed down the left. **Templates** holds video and poster templates. PDF looks are under Settings → Production defaults and Create's PDF choice, Pronunciation is under Settings → Voices, and What worked on YouTube is on the channel page. Old addresses still open the right page.
- The channel picker reads **Showing: All channels** in the header of Home, Projects and Calendar, and only appears with two or more channels.

## Your first video

- The welcome is a checklist of what a short needs (Article, Narration, Images), each with its state and one button to fix it. The usage notice is a single line at the bottom of the window instead of a dialog, and start at login is asked once.
- **Create is one screen.** Pick what you're making, type the topic (the box has the cursor already) and press **Make the video**. Each part of the run is one sentence with Change, and anything missing is named once, with Fix. On a phone the Make button stays pinned within reach.
- **New prompt** opens beside Create and picks the prompt when you save, so you never leave the page. If you skipped the starter templates, Create offers them.
- **Everything this run will do** shows the whole run in plain sentences before you start.

## A finished project

- The video, its downloads, the chapters, the shorts and every stage's status are on the first screen. Each section (Article, Narration, Images, Video, Shorts, YouTube, PDF, All files) leads with what it made and a named download.
- **Edit settings** uses Create's sections and names. **Versions** replaces History.
- Every action is a visible button: Mark uploaded, Rename, Save as template and Delete. Paid work says so on its button ("Make this image again").

## Lists and settings

- **Home** shows your finished videos first, with Watch and Download, then what needs you and what's running, step by step.
- **Projects** rows show a picture, the length, what was made and when, with Open and Download.
- **Templates** are cards that say what each one makes, with **Make a video** and **Edit template**. Edit template's sections match Create's.
- **Prompts** searches every kind at once. The editor reads like text, **Insert a keyword** shows exactly what goes in, and every kind has examples to start from.
- **Settings** is one list of pages. **Keys & services** opens on three lines (who writes, who reads, who draws), each Ready or with a button to set it up. Trash has its own page, and Projects and Prompts show **Trash (n)** when something is in it.

## One word per thing

- Captions (not subtitles), Narration, Voice, Images, Make … again (not Remake or Regenerate), Copy for YouTube Studio, Pauses and checks, PDF looks, and US spelling throughout.
- Info buttons are gone where a short line under the setting says it better. A status dot always has its word next to it, and each area has at most one lime button.

## Fixes

- Typing the topic the moment Create opens no longer drops the starter template's writer and image service.
- Make the video is ready about a second after you stop typing, instead of three or four.
