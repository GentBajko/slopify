# Slopify 3.1.0

Released 30 September 2026.

3.1.0 lets a schedule's videos be made ahead of their day, and several videos from one Start run together.

## Highlights

- **Prepare a scheduled video ahead.** Everything but the video is made now; its scheduled day only renders.
- **Run several videos at once.** Play's Queue switch: on, one after another; off, all together.

## Schedules and the calendar

- Every queued run on the calendar has **Prepare**. Slopify makes the project now, exactly as the schedule would on the day (the template as it is now, the topic and the schedule's values), and runs research, the article, narration, images, the thumbnail and the PDF. It holds before the video, and the run shows **Prepared** with **Open**.
- On the scheduled day the schedule continues that project instead of making another: the video, its captions, shorts and YouTube description are made. An unfinished project you made yourself under the same title is continued the same way; a finished or canceled one is not.
- Open a prepared project to read the article or listen to the narration, and change anything before its day. A Before Video checkpoint the template already has still waits for you.

## Play

- With more than one video, the Start box has a **Queue** switch (on by default). Off, they all start at once as separate runs, and the Play key reads **Start N videos**. At once finishes sooner while steps wait on providers, but the renders share your processor and plan limits are reached sooner.

## Fixes

- **Review** refused any setup with burned-in captions and a Before Video checkpoint ("Something changed since you reviewed this video"), however often you pressed it.

## Upgrading from 3.0.15

- **Nothing becomes outdated.** The database gains one table, for prepared videos.
