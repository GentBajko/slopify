# Slopify 3.6.3

Released 3 October 2026.

## Subtitles

- **Names with ø, æ, ß, ð, þ or ł no longer stop the video.** Local subtitle timing read a name like Asbjørnsen as a foreign script and failed the Video step. Those Latin letters are now read as their plain spelling (ø as o, æ as ae, ß as ss…). Other scripts, such as Cyrillic, still need another subtitle provider.

## Upgrading

- A video that stopped with "Local subtitles currently support English speech and Latin-script names only" because of such a name: press **Retry** on its Video step.
