# Slopify 3.0.10

Released 29 September 2026.

3.0.10 makes a project's page fit the window, so everything about a project is in view at once.

## Highlights

- **The project page fits the window.** No more scrolling the whole page to find a section's text.

## Project page

- On screens 1181 px and wider the page itself no longer scrolls. The title stays at the top, and the section list, the open section and the status rail each scroll on their own.
- The article reads straight down its section instead of in a box inside the page, and its search stays in view while you read. Its buttons, tabs and text start higher.
- YouTube's parts (description, tags, pinned comment, other titles) fold, one open at a time; each keeps its Copy and its count on its heading.
- Shorts sit three across instead of two.
- The status rail is shorter: one line per step of the run, free space with a "Free 683 MB" button, and the cost as one line with "By stage". The cost line above the sections is gone.
- "The video is ready" is shown once, in the status rail, not repeated inside the YouTube section.
- Live: once the article is written it says so, with Read it, instead of waiting for text; the images are shown in their order; the steps are left to the status rail.
- Waveform players draw one bar per 4 pixels, so a long narration no longer blurs into a block, and a narrow player drops its volume slider (the mute button stays) to give the waveform the room.

## Upgrading from 3.0.9

- **Nothing becomes outdated.**
