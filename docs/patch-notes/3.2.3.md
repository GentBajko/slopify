# Slopify 3.2.3

Released 1 October 2026.

3.2.3 makes Soften and retry actually reword a short's refused picture, and shows a render's progress on Home.

## Shorts

- **Soften and retry** on a short's picture now draws the reworded prompt. In 3.2.2 the button appeared, but the picture was asked for with its original wording again and was refused again.

## Home

- **Running now** shows what a running step is doing and how far it is ("Rendering the video (45%)"), as the project page does, instead of a step count that stood still through the whole render.

## Thumbnails

- Regenerating a thumbnail asks with its own sentence: it makes a new thumbnail and leaves the video and shorts alone. It used the image's wording, which said the video would be rendered again.

## Upgrading from 3.2.2

- **Nothing becomes outdated.**
