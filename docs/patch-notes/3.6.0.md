# Slopify 3.6.0

Released 3 October 2026.

## Channels → YouTube numbers

- **Import from Studio.** Export Studio's Advanced mode (Analytics → Advanced mode, choose your metrics as columns, the Export arrow → Comma-separated values) and import the zip it downloads. Every video becomes a row with every metric you chose: views, average view duration, percentage viewed, RPM, revenue, watch time, subscribers, impressions, click-through rate, and anything else Studio offers.
- **Compare at a glance.** Order the videos by any column, show or hide columns (Slopify remembers your choice in this browser), and see each video's daily figure for the chart's metric as a small line. Videos Slopify made link to their projects; your older videos are listed too.
- A new import replaces the old one.
