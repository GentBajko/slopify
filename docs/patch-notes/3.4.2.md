# Slopify 3.4.2

Released 2 October 2026.

## YouTube Studio extension (1.1.1)

- **Your Studio numbers arrive.** A daily read that found nothing to read (Slopify not reachable, or no video linked yet) no longer waits a whole day: it tries again at the next check, 15 minutes later.
- **Read Studio numbers now**, at the foot of the extension's popup, reads every linked video's views, CTR and average view duration at once, one background tab after another. They show in the Projects list and on each project's On YouTube.

## Upgrading

- **Download the extension again** (Settings → YouTube Studio → Download) and reload it in `chrome://extensions`. It is version 1.1.1.
