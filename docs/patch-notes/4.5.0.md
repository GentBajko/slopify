# Slopify 4.5.0

Released 18 November 2026.

Long videos now come out AV1 on an NVIDIA graphics card, about half the size of before for the same picture, and every template can choose its own video codec.

## Smaller videos: AV1 on the graphics card by default

- **The long video is now encoded as AV1 on the NVIDIA card wherever the card can do it,** whatever Settings → Production defaults → Video encoder says. Measured on a real 3-minute stretch of a 1080p video (slow pan and zoom over dense engraving), the card's AV1 file is about 44% of the size of the H.264 one for the same picture (about 94.4 VMAF against it).
- **Without a card, or on a card that can't encode AV1, nothing changes:** the video is made exactly as before, as H.264 through the Video encoder setting, and nothing is said about it.
- **If the card's AV1 encode fails mid-render,** the video is made again the old way.
- **No finished video turns outdated.** A video comes out AV1 the next time it is made for another reason.

## Choose a video codec per template

- **Video codec is a new setting in the Video section of Create, the template editor and Edit settings:** Automatic (the default: AV1 on the graphics card, else H.264), H.264, HEVC or AV1.
- **H.264** keeps a video exactly as before, never AV1.
- **HEVC and AV1** make a much smaller file. AV1 on the processor (SVT-AV1) is about 29% of the H.264 size but renders slower and needs an ffmpeg with SVT-AV1 on the computer; AV1 on the card is about 44%; HEVC on the card about 59%. HEVC on the processor (x265) is not measured yet.
- **A video is never failed for its codec.** When the chosen codec isn't available, the video is made as H.264 and the project's Video part says so and where to change it.
- **Only the long video changes.** Shorts and the style preview stay H.264, for the services shorts go to.
