## Channels → YouTube numbers

- **A chart for each video.** Under the table, every video from Studio's export gets its own chart from its first day: its own scale, dates along the bottom, the value on hover, with its title and total. It follows the comparison chart's choice of per day, 7-day average or running total, and its period.
- Studio's export holds daily figures only for the videos drawn in its own chart; the others can't be ticked for charts, and the page says how to include them.
