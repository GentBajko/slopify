# Slopify 3.22.0

Released 13 October 2026.

- **Names keep their pronunciation in possessives.** "Gygax's", "Lolth's" and every other possessive of a name in the Pronunciation Glossary were read without the glossary's pronunciation. They now get it, with the right ending sound added.
- **Hyphenated names work in the glossary.** Names like "Arach-Tinilith" or "Catti-brie" with one pronunciation per part were refused; they are now used.
- **Blank glossary entries are no longer reported as errors.** When the article leaves a name's pronunciation blank ("—"), the Pronunciation tab now says how many names are read as written, instead of listing them as broken entries.
- **A project's page no longer scrolls into an empty screen.** On wide screens, scrolling outside the columns of a project (most of all on Video) could slide the whole page up past its content.

## Upgrading from 3.21.0

- The new glossary rules apply to projects made from now on. Projects already made keep the old rules, so nothing already voiced becomes outdated.
