# Slopify 4.5.2

Released 18 November 2026.

A new intro or outro no longer makes a video's shorts again, and the AI's notes about its own work no longer open an article.

## Shorts stay when the intro or outro changes

- **Shorts are now cut on the narration body's own clock.** A new intro or outro (its prompt, text or narration) no longer marks the shorts outdated: their picks, image prompts, pictures and renders stay as they are.
- **Only a short whose own words changed is picked again.** Rewriting another chapter or voicing another chunk again leaves every other short as it is. A short with a changed word gets a new moment in its own number, instead of the pick stopping with an error.
- **A short never reaches outside the body:** no intro or silence at its start, and it ends where the body's audio ends.
- **Existing projects keep their shorts.** The first change that moves the timing picks again once, keeping the finished shorts by their sentences, and only renders them again.
- **An edit that sends an empty keep list no longer drops every short.** Projects already re-picked before this keep their new shorts; the old ones come back only by restoring the version before that change.

## No production notes in articles

- **Short opening paragraphs where the AI talks about its own work** ("I've read all sixteen documents and am now writing…") are now removed from the article before its first heading, and from the first paragraph of an AI-written intro or outro, so they are never narrated.
