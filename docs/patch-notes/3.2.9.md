# Slopify 3.2.9

Released 2 October 2026.

## Projects

- **Renaming a project no longer makes everything again.** The pictures, scenes, looks and shorts stay as they are: they keep what the project was about. What shows the title follows the new name: the YouTube description and other titles, the PDF and the audio files' tags.

## Upgrading from 3.2.8

- **Nothing becomes outdated.** A project renamed before this version keeps whatever its rename already queued.
