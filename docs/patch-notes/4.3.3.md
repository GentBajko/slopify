# Slopify 4.3.3

Released 9 November 2026.

A video with many images no longer stops the app while it decides what to run next.

## The run stops blocking the app

- **Every time a run asked itself what to start next, it worked the answer out from scratch for each waiting step** — reading the whole revision back off disk, a check for every output and every picture, the article read again, and every step re-derived from the narration up. With twelve pictures that happened a few dozen times across a run and nobody saw it. With 240 it happened tens of thousands of times, one core went to nothing but that, and the app stopped answering: pages hung, and nothing could be opened until it was restarted.
- **It is now worked out once for each sweep and shared by every step in it.** A sweep starts with nothing carried over from the one before, so a step that has just finished is still seen the moment it finishes.
- **Nothing about what runs, or in what order, has changed** — only how often the same answer is worked out.
