# Slopify 3.18.1

Released 11 October 2026.

- **Your images are kept with your video.** Every image the video shows now sits in the project's `Upload/Images/` folder, in order, and each short's images sit beside that short in `Upload/Shorts/<n> - <title>/`. They used to be under `Working/`.
- **Cleanup no longer deletes images.** **Keep outputs only**, **Free space** and **Clean up everything** keep the video's and shorts' images now. Before, they deleted them as working files, and making them again never gives the same pictures.

## Upgrading from 3.18.0

- On the first start, finished projects move their images from `Working/` into `Upload/` by themselves. Nothing is copied, so this takes no extra space.
- Images an earlier cleanup already deleted can't be brought back by this update. A backup made before the cleanup still has them; otherwise remaking the images makes new ones.
