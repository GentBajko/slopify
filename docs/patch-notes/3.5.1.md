# Slopify 3.5.1

Released 2 October 2026.

## Channels → YouTube numbers

- **A channel's YouTube numbers in one place.** A new **YouTube numbers** tab on each channel shows its long videos' and its shorts' totals (views, impressions, click-through rate, average view duration, watch hours), then every video with its shorts under it, ordered newest first, by views, by click-through rate or by average view.
- The numbers are what Studio showed when the Slopify Studio extension last read them: daily while Chrome is open, or at once with **Read Studio numbers now** in its popup.

## Calendar → Releases

- **"Checks clear" for real.** Studio shows a clear video as "No notices · reaching viewers and earning"; Slopify now reads that as clear instead of as a problem.
