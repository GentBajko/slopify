# Slopify 3.0.5

Released 28 September 2026.

3.0.5 says what a running video is doing in plain words, adds a clock for how long Slopify worked on a project, and gives the browser extension Slopify's own icon.

## Highlights

- **Clear video progress**: "Rendering the video (82%)" instead of "10.159999999999998 of 50".
- **A work clock** on every project: how long Slopify spent working, never the time spent waiting on you.
- **Slopify's icon** in the browser extension.

## Project page

- A running Video stage names the step it is on and counts that step alone: "Timing the captions (30%)", "Drawing the shorts' pictures (12 of 38)", "Rendering the video (82%)". The count used to add every short's pictures and renders once the shorts were picked, so it jumped from 9 of 11 to 10 of 50 and the bar went backwards.
- While a project runs, the top of its page shows **Working for 9 min 30 s**, ticking while a step runs. While it is paused or waits on a review or a plan limit, the clock holds still and reads "9 min 30 s of work so far".
- When a run ends, the cost line adds how long it worked: "This run cost $2.73 · 22 min 15 s of work". Steps that run side by side, such as narration and images, count once.
- The **Cost** section shows this run's working time, each stage's working time and the total over every run. The old "end to end" time was often wrong: it added up stages that ran at the same time.

## Home

- Running projects show whole numbers in their progress, never long decimals.

## Browser extension

- The toolbar button and the browser's extensions page show Slopify's icon, not a plain letter.

## Upgrading from 3.0.4

- **Nothing becomes outdated.** Past projects get their working time from the steps they already ran.
