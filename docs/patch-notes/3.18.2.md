# Slopify 3.18.2

Released 11 October 2026.

- **Each image is named by its scene.** In a project's Images, every image is titled by its own scene, for example "Explorers scramble along a rock shelf above a fossil sea…", instead of the prompt's opening, which was the same on every image.
- **The full-size view says the prompt once.** The scene is on the top bar and the whole prompt is under the picture, instead of the whole prompt in both places.

## Upgrading from 3.18.1

- Nothing becomes outdated; existing images get their titles at once.
