# Slopify 3.8.2

Released 4 October 2026.

- **Shorts of a video already on YouTube get release times.** A project whose long video went up before it had a release time left its shorts with none, so the Studio extension had no schedule to type in. Now the long video keeps the time it went up as its release, and its shorts take the release line's short times from the upload lead on (never due sooner than you could upload them).
- **A narration chunk recorded again by itself no longer gets stuck.** When the voice garbled words, Slopify promised to record the chunk again and carry on, but if the project's last other step was just finishing, the retry waited for a step that never came, and the video stayed failed. It now looks again every 15 seconds until the project is free.
