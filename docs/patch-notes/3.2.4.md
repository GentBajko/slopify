# Slopify 3.2.4

Released 1 October 2026.

3.2.4 keeps a title's fixed wording in the A/B titles, and lets a finished run with a leftover step leave Needs you.

## YouTube

- When a project's title comes from a pattern with keywords ("{{Topic}} | My Channel"), the two other titles for YouTube's title A/B test change only what the keywords hold ("Tiamat, the Five-Headed Dragon Queen | My Channel"). An answer that changes the rest is asked for again. Titles without keywords keep free alternatives.
- Projects made from a schedule keep their title pattern too.

## Home

- **Keep as is** on a Needs you card whose run waits on held work: the project leaves Needs you without running anything, for example a step added after the project was made whose run would redraw finished thumbnails. Undo is in the message; editing the project brings the card back. Projects lists it as "Kept as is", and it can show under Ready to upload.

## Upgrading from 3.2.3

- **Nothing becomes outdated.** The A/B titles change applies to projects made from now on.
