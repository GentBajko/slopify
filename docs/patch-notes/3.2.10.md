# Slopify 3.2.10

Released 2 October 2026.

## Projects

- **Renaming a project runs nothing.** Every step keeps what the project was made about, so a new name never rewrites the outro, re-records it or renders the video again (3.2.9 kept only the pictures). Press **Write again** in the YouTube section if you want the description written for the new name.

## YouTube Studio extension

- **Click the extension's icon** to see your finished projects that aren't marked uploaded. Open one for its video and shorts (a ✓ marks those on YouTube), and click one to upload it: YouTube Studio's upload page opens, and the extension adds the file and fills in the details. Pairing is a link in the popup's corner.
- **An upload counts only once Studio confirms it** ("Video scheduled", "Video published" or "Video saved"). One you cancel, or close as a draft, isn't marked on YouTube, stays offered in the popup ("Upload again") and gets no A/B test.

- **Links fill themselves in.** Open YouTube Studio's Content page and the extension matches its videos and shorts to your projects by title, so uploads you made by hand get their links in Slopify too.
- **The shorts link the full video by themselves:** once Slopify knows the long video's link, each short's "Watch the full video" line uses it, instead of "Link needed".

## Upgrading from 3.2.9

- **Download the extension again** (Settings → YouTube Studio → Download), unzip it over the old one and press reload in `chrome://extensions`. It is version 0.4.0.
- **Nothing becomes outdated.** A video the extension recorded with 3.2.8 or 3.2.9 counts as on YouTube.
