# Slopify 4.5.1

Released 18 November 2026.

The Images step now counts its pictures and their checks apart, so a video of 240 pictures no longer reads "259 of 496".

## Pictures and checks counted once each

- **With the picture check on, the Images row says "234 of 240 pictures · 43 of 240 checked"** on the project page, in the run's step list and under Home's pictures. Before, every picture and every check counted as one step together, so 240 pictures showed as about 496.
- **The time left still covers both:** the drawing and the checking still to do.
- **Without the picture check,** the row says only how many pictures are drawn.
