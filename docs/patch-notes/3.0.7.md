# Slopify 3.0.7

Released 28 September 2026.

3.0.7 makes schedules show the template version they really use, adds Literata to PDF themes, and keeps pinned comments short.

## Highlights

- **Schedules show the template they run.** Editing a schedule shows each template at its newest version.
- **Literata for PDFs**, a text face made for long reading.
- **Short pinned comments:** a line or two, not paragraphs.
- **New project** in the sidebar.

## Schedules

- Edit schedule lists every template at its newest version, the one every run uses, and saving records it. It used to show the version the schedule was first saved with ("· v3") and offer no other, even though runs already used the newest.

## PDF themes

- PDF themes can use **Literata** in regular, bold, italic and bold italic: a text face made for long reading on screens.

## YouTube description

- The pinned comment is one or two short sentences (about 200 characters): a question viewers can answer and an ask for the next topic. It was two to four paragraphs.

## Everywhere

- **New video** is now **New project**, in the sidebar, on Home and Projects, and as the `C` command: a project can be an audiobook, a podcast or a PDF as well as a video.
- ElevenLabs lists **Eleven v4** and **Eleven v4 Turbo** even before the model list is checked.

## Upgrading from 3.0.6

- **Nothing becomes outdated.** To see a schedule's newest template version, open it and press **Save changes** once.
