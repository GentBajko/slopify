# Slopify 4.5.4

Released 21 November 2026.

A video made ahead waits for its day again after a restart, instead of reading Stopped.

## A video made ahead no longer reads Stopped after a restart and Continue

- **A video made ahead waits for its day again after Continue the run.** When the app restarted while a schedule was making a video ahead, the video could read Stopped, with Continue the run offered, even after pressing Continue the run or Try again: a step that only stood in for the narration until the article was written stayed on the video after its narration was voiced, the restart held it, and Continue had nothing to start it with. Such a video would not have been rendered on its day. That leftover step is now dropped as soon as the narration it stood for is planned, and a video already stuck this way reads Waiting for its day again after one more Continue the run. Nothing is voiced, drawn or paid for again, and every checkpoint still waits for its approval.
- Technical: `materializeAdmittedWork` (`rebuild/runtime-materialize.ts`) removes the head's reservation of an admitted, never-started `:future` placeholder of an unfinished stage once its version's plan no longer defers that key and every step it anchors (`futureKey`) is reserved on the head, held or allowed alike. Before, such a placeholder was carried into each new version (`preserveDeferredIntent`), held by `recoverWork` at boot, and never admitted again by Continue, so `heldBesideReviews` (`reviews/holds.ts`) kept the run stopped.
